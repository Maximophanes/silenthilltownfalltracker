How the ending calculation works

The normal endings use a points system. The rules below are based on community reverse-engineering of the game’s Unreal Engine blueprints rather than official Konami documentation.

Reverse-engineered

Discoveries · +1 each

Up to 85 points. Documents, Clues, and emails read on computers count. Postcards do not, because they are profile-level collectibles rather than save-level scoring Discoveries.

Qualifying Simon kills · −2 each

Only Simon’s kills of Motionless, Sorrowful, Lunger (Fearful), and Widow (Enraged) count. Fallen, The Weight (The Wall), and enemies killed while playing as Richard do not. The end-of-game Enemies Killed total includes non-scoring kills, so it should not be entered here.

Cannula refills · −4 each

Each cannula refill/revive costs 4 points. Other healing items do not affect the ending score.

Zoe mistakes · −3 each

Wrong vital-sign readings, an incorrect pump setup, and failed intubation steps in The Patient count. With the pumps set incorrectly, another mistake can be registered roughly every 5 seconds while Zoe remains in the surgery room; the timer pauses when she leaves the room.

New Game+ special-ending branches

Dog and Sci-Fi can both be fully prepared on the same New Game+ playthrough. After viewing all three Strange Books, using the Strange Access Card at the Part 6 keypad triggers Sci-Fi before the final boss. If you skip the card reader and all three coins have been inserted, Dog occurs after the final boss. To obtain both from the same setup, make a manual save before the keypad, take one branch, then reload and take the other.
